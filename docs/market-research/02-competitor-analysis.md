# 02 — Competitor Analysis

Evidence labels: **[V]** verified · **[R]** reported · **[A]** assumption. Links are in [sources.md](sources.md).

> **Important:** almost all competitor information below is **[R] reported**. It comes from vendor marketing, app-store listings or software directories seen through search results; vendor pages could not be opened directly. **Prices and features must be checked on each vendor's own page before we rely on them.**
>
> "Not found" means our research found no information. It does **not** mean the feature or weakness doesn't exist. Weaknesses are listed only where a source reports them.

---

## 1. Overview

The market is **crowded**. There are international free POS apps, a Nigerian commerce platform active in the region, several Ghanaian bookkeeping apps, and many small Ghanaian POS and inventory products, several of them recent. No single dominant app for Ghanaian provision stores was identified.

**Main implication:** ShopMate will not win on having a unique feature list. It must win on **simplicity, reliability offline, trust in data safety, local support and price**. Those must be tested, not assumed.

## 3. Observed price range in Ghana (all [R])

| Band | Examples |
|---|---|
| Free | Loyverse core, Zoho POS free plan, Bumpa Basic, ArmPOS free version, CliqPOS free version, Clink free |
| About GH₵17–40/month | IsoPOS (GH₵200/year ≈ GH₵17/month), Clink Pro (GH₵25), Ree ($4.99), Bumpa Starter (estimated about GH₵43) |
| About GH₵90–200/month | HilsonPOS (GH₵90), OceanCyber (GH₵99), MyBiz (GH₵149, vendor claim), Bumpa Pro and Growth (estimated) |
| Per transaction | Hubtel (about 1.95% reported) |

**Implication:** our earlier pricing hypothesis of GH₵50–80/month sits between the cheap and mid bands, while **strong free options exist**. Price sensitivity is a key test (see `04-validation-plan.md`). A free tier or low entry price looks necessary to compete **[A]**.

## 4. Gaps we could address (hypotheses, not facts)

Each gap is a **hypothesis** **[A]**, based on what we did or didn't find. It must be confirmed through review mining and hands-on testing.

| Possible gap | Why we think it may exist | How to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| **Combination** of offline sales + stock + credit ledger + true profit in one simple, cedi-first Android app | Credit tracking is absent in Loyverse [R] and is split into separate debt-only apps (Clink) [R]; offline support is unconfirmed for most Ghanaian POS products | Hands-on test of 3–5 apps with the scorecard |
| **Reliable, visible sync** | Loyverse reviewers complain about sync lag [R] | Review mining; our own sync status design |
| **Correct handling of negative stock** | Loyverse reviewers note negative inventory is allowed without clear handling [R] | Our discrepancy-flag design |
| **Data trust: export and continuity** | Kippa's shutdown left users without data [V] | Survey question on data-loss worries |
| **Owner-friendly simplicity** (less "accounting", more "my shop today") | Research shows skills gaps and resistance to complex software [V]; many competitors are full POS/ERP systems [R] | Hands-on testing; pilot task-completion time |
| **Local, human support (WhatsApp)** | OZÉ found in-person support vital [V] | Pilot retention with WhatsApp support |
| **Affordable price with honest limits** | Many local products are GH₵90+/month [R]; free ones may lack credit or local fit [R] | Survey willingness to pay; pilot conversion |

## 5. What we must not claim

- That competitors "don't work offline" (not established for most).
- That competitors are "too expensive" (several are free or cheap).
- That there is "no app for Ghanaian shops" (there are many).

## 6. Recommended next step

The project owner should install and test **3–5 competitor apps** on an Android phone, for about 1–2 hours in total, using [templates/competitor-test-scorecard.md](templates/competitor-test-scorecard.md). Suggested apps: **Loyverse, Zoho POS, Bumpa, OZÉ (if available) and one Ghanaian POS app (e.g. CliqPOS or MyPayAid)**. This is free and gives first-hand evidence that this research could not.
