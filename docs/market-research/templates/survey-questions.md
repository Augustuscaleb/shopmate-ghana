# Online Survey — Shop Records in Ghana (draft)

**Tool:** Google Forms (free). **Length:** about 5 minutes. **Share via:** WhatsApp.

---

**Intro text (top of the form)**

> Hello! We are building a simple phone app to help small shops in Ghana track sales, stock, customers who owe, and profit. This short survey (about 5 minutes) helps us understand what shop owners really need. Your answers are confidential and used only for this research. Contact details are optional. Thank you!
>
> ☐ I agree to take part (required)

## Section A — About your shop

1. Do you own or run a shop? *(Yes, I own it / Yes, I manage it / No → end the survey with thanks)*
2. What type of shop? *(Provision store / Mini-mart / Boutique / Cosmetics / Pharmacy/chemist / Phone & accessories / Other: ___)*
3. Which region and town? *(text)*
4. How many people work in the shop, including you? *(Just me / 2 / 3–5 / 6+)*
5. About how many different products do you sell? *(Under 50 / 50–200 / 200–500 / 500+)*
6. About how many sales (customers) do you serve on a normal day? *(Under 20 / 20–50 / 50–100 / 100+)*

## Section B — How you keep records today

7. How do you record your sales? *(I don't record them / Notebook / Excel or phone notes / An app — which? ___ / Other)*
8. How do you know when to restock? *(I look at the shelves / Notebook / App / Supplier tells me / Other)*
9. How do you know your profit for the month? *(I don't really know / Rough estimate / Calculate from notebook / App or accountant)*
10. Which is the **biggest headache** in running your shop? *(Pick up to 2: Knowing my profit / Customers who owe me / Running out of stock / Too much stock / Cash and MoMo not matching sales / Tracking expenses / Staff honesty / Other: ___)*

## Section C — Credit and payments

11. Do customers buy on credit ("book") from you? *(Often / Sometimes / Rarely / Never)*
12. If yes, how do you track who owes you? *(Memory / Notebook / Phone notes / App / Other)*
13. Have you lost money because a customer didn't pay or the record was lost? *(Yes, often / Yes, sometimes / No)*
14. Roughly what share of your sales are paid by MoMo? *(Almost none / About a quarter / About half / Most)*

## Section D — Phone and internet

15. What phone do you use for the business? *(Android smartphone / iPhone / Basic phone (no apps) / I don't use a phone for business)*
16. How is your internet at the shop? *(Always fine / Sometimes goes off / Often bad / I rarely use data)*
17. Do you have an email address you use? *(Yes, regularly / Yes, but rarely / No)*

## Section E — Interest and price

18. If a simple app recorded your sales, stock and credit customers and showed your daily profit, would you try it? *(Definitely / Probably / Not sure / No)*
19. What would worry you about using such an app? *(Pick any: Too complicated / Data cost / Losing my records / Privacy / Takes too long at the counter / Cost / Nothing / Other)*
20. If it saved you time and money, how much would you pay **per month**? *(Only if free / GH₵10–20 / GH₵21–40 / GH₵41–60 / GH₵61–100 / More than GH₵100)*
21. Have you tried a shop or bookkeeping app before? Which one, and why did you stop? *(text, optional)*

## Section F — Follow-up (optional)

22. Would you like to test the app for free when it's ready? *(Yes / No)*
23. If yes, your name and WhatsApp number *(optional — used only to contact you about the pilot)*

---

**Analysis plan:**
- Filter to shop owners/managers (Q1).
- Count: headaches (Q10), credit prevalence (Q11–13), phone type (Q15), email use (Q17), interest (Q18) and price bands (Q20).
- Compare provision stores vs boutiques vs mini-marts.
- Store contact details separately and delete them when the pilot ends.
