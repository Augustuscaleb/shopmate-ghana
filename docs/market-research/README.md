## Evidence ratings used throughout

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[V] Verified** | Official statistics, peer-reviewed research, or reputable news reporting of official data (e.g. Ghana Statistical Service, Bank of Ghana) |
| **[R] Reported** | Secondary or self-reported information: vendor websites, app-store listings, directories, trade-association statements, single news reports. Probably true, but not independently confirmed |
| **[A] Assumption** | Our reasoning or belief, not yet supported by evidence. **Must be tested.** |

## Method and limitations

- Research used web search over publicly available sources (statistical releases, academic papers, news, vendor pages, software review sites).
- **Limitation:** in this research environment, vendor websites and app-store pages could not be opened directly, only seen through search results. Competitor prices and features are therefore rated **[R]** and must be checked on the vendor's own page before being relied on. App-store reviews could not be read directly. A review-mining method for doing this manually is in `04-validation-plan.md`.
- No interviews, surveys or app tests have been carried out yet.
- Figures can change. Each source is listed with its date where known.
